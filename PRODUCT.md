# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users
Everyday Solana users who want to know whether an address or token is safe before they send money, and are not security experts. Scam victims who want to report what happened. Wallets and trading platforms evaluating the scam graph API. Hackathon judges reading the page to understand the product in under a minute.

## Product Purpose
Ladon maps wallets belonging to scammers, drainers and serial rug pullers, and warns people before they pay them. The site explains that promise, gets people to install the extension, and makes clear the data is open through a public API. Success is a visitor who understands what Ladon does, trusts that it is careful, and installs it or follows the build.

## Brand Personality
Watchful, calm, trustworthy. A guardian that never sleeps, not a siren. Classical gravity from the myth, with pixel detail that nods to the crypto native audience. Warnings are the one loud moment.

## Anti-references
- A reskin of the Crypto World's Fair site: take the painting plus pixel type approach, not its layout, purple, nav or seal.
- Crypto security sites with neon on black, shields, padlocks and hacker green.
- SaaS landing templates: stat rows, identical feature card grids, gradient text.
- Fear mongering copy or red alarm styling outside the warning itself.

## Design Principles
1. Show the mechanism. The linked wallet graph is the insight, so the page shows it, not just claims it.
2. Probabilities, not accusations. Every score is framed with its confidence and reason.
3. Calm until it matters. Restraint everywhere except the warning, so the warning lands.
4. Plain English. Written for someone who just wants to know if an address is safe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at least 4.5:1. Visible keyboard focus. Every scroll animation has a reduced motion alternative, and all content is readable without the animation.
